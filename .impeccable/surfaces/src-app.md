# treader: app frame and reader

Scope: the whole app. Reader and book pages are Read mode; /me, /write and /admin are Operate mode inside the same world. Audience: phone readers of serious fiction, and writers publishing chapter by chapter. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (protection, private notes, phones first).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every book is a clothbound volume in its own colour, and reading happens on its plain paper pages. It refuses the feed of thumbnail cards on white and the blanket cream-and-serif page: colour lives on the boards, never in the text.

OWN-WORLD: Flat bookcloth colours, one per book (oxblood, bottle green, indigo, ochre, slate, rust, plum, teal), on a warm paper column with dark ink. Titles are stamped: Literata caps, letterspaced, gilt (#C9A24A) or blind (tone on tone) on the cloth. A silk ribbon in the book's colour marks where you stopped. Read chapters get a pencil tick, and finished books end on a stamped THE END. Controls are flat, with hairline rules, the system sans, and no shadows except the board's edge.

STORY: The reader sees a shelf of the author's boards, opens one, and reads in stillness. The tap-revealed controls, ribbon and notes return them to the exact place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a phone, a profile shows the author's name and a short bio, then a shelf of cloth boards two across at a 2:3 aspect, each with a stamped title and a small status (Ongoing or Complete). In a chapter: a 6px cloth headband, the chapter number in the margin column, the title, then text at 18px with a 65-character line. The ribbon hangs at the top right, and the controls stay hidden until a tap.

FORM: Bookcloth and Stamp, position 3 on the grounded list. seed:73a61a07e5caad77. Raises: colour only at the edges (cloud edge); state as a mark, not a hue (cutting bench); stillness while reading (cracktro); numbers in one strict margin column (crane).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
